The Company

Swiss watchmaking expertise, built around your identity.

Founded in 2007, Wonderful Times SA is a Swiss company based in Alle. We specialize in sourcing watch movements and customizing watch components for established brands, emerging labels and independent creators.

Our extensive network and industry expertise allow us to select the right partners for each project. From the first technical discussion to the final finish, every solution is tailored to the brand, production volume and creative direction.

Traditional Decorations

The visual language of fine watchmaking.

Traditional techniques bring depth, rhythm and light to bridges, plates, rotors and visible components.

01

Côtes de Genève

Parallel decorative waves creating structured reflections across bridges and rotors.

02

Perlage

Overlapping circular graining traditionally used on plates and concealed surfaces.

03

Anglage

Refined beveling that defines component edges and catches light with precision.

04

Sunray brushing

Radial lines extending from a center point for a dynamic, luminous effect.

05

Snailing

Spiral finishing commonly applied to wheels, barrels and selected circular parts.

06

Other finishes

We can discuss any type of decorative finishing to achieve refined, high-end results tailored to your project.

07

Sandblasted finish

Fine abrasive blasting creates a uniform matte texture with a refined, technical appearance.

08

Classic engraving

Logotypes, lettering and ornamental motifs integrated into the movement architecture.
